Title: Safe storage and the temperature danger zone
Source: https://health.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/interactive-guide.html?l=3
Accessed: 2026-11-11
Status: Summary — paraphrased from the source, not a verbatim quote.

Set the refrigerator to 4 degrees Celsius (40 degrees Fahrenheit) or lower, and the freezer to minus 18 degrees Celsius (0 degrees Fahrenheit) or lower. Check the temperature with an appliance thermometer rather than trusting the dial.

The temperature danger zone is between 4 degrees Celsius (40 degrees Fahrenheit) and 60 degrees Celsius (140 degrees Fahrenheit). Bacteria can grow quickly in that range, so keep cold food cold and hot food hot.

Refrigerate or freeze perishable food, prepared food, and leftovers within two hours. Cooked meat, stews, and egg or vegetable dishes keep in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Cooked poultry and fish keep in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Do not pack the refrigerator so full that cold air cannot circulate.

Keep hot foods at 60 degrees Celsius (140 degrees Fahrenheit) or above. When in doubt about whether food has been stored safely, throw it out.
